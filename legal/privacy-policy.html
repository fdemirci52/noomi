Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Noomi ("we", "us") is operated by Mehmet Fatih Demirci, an independent developer (stvy.io). Questions about this policy: hello@noomiapp.site.

This is a plain-language policy. If anything here contradicts what the app actually does, that is a bug on our side — tell us and we will fix it.

What we collect

Your account. When you first open Noomi we create an anonymous account so your taste profile has somewhere to live. If you sign in with Apple we store the identifier Apple gives us and the email address Apple shares — which may be a private relay address if you chose to hide your real one.

What you tell us about your taste. Ratings, the tags you pick, notes you write, your watchlist and watched list, titles you mark as not interested or dropped, and the answers you give during onboarding: the name you want us to use, your country, your age range, what you want from Noomi, the languages you watch in, how you feel about subtitles and the topics you're interested in. Your answer to the gender question and your profile photo stay on your device; we do not receive them. This is the product; there is no version of Noomi that works without it.

How you use the app. Which titles you open, how long a card stays on screen, which recommendation you acted on, and which screen an action came from, along with your device type (phone or tablet) and iOS version. We use this to tell whether the recommendations are actually good.

What you ask the advisor. Messages you send to the AI advisor and the context we send with them (your taste profile and candidate titles).

Your preferences. Region, streaming services, content interests, and notification settings.

Subscription status. Whether you have an active subscription, handled through RevenueCat. We never see your payment details — Apple handles payment.

Crash diagnostics. When the app crashes we receive a technical report (device model, OS version, and where in the code it failed). These are not linked to your account.

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads and we do not embed advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.

Where your data lives

On our servers in Singapore (Supabase, AWS region ap-southeast-1) and locally on your device. Data in transit is encrypted.

How long we keep it

Your ratings, library, taste profile and advisor history: as long as your account exists. Usage data (how you use the app, and the log of AI requests we make for you) is kept for up to 13 months, then deleted; only anonymous daily totals remain.

When you delete your account we erase everything linked to you from our own servers immediately, and it cannot be undone. Records of what AI requests and purchases cost us are kept only after your identity has been permanently removed from them. Aggregated statistics that cannot be traced back to you may remain.

One thing deletion cannot reach right away. Anything that was sent to our AI provider — your advisor messages, pasted viewing history, and taste summaries — also sits on their systems for a short period, and deleting your Noomi account does not shorten it. Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days of receiving or generating them. If a request is flagged under their usage policy, they may keep it for up to two years. Anthropic does not use commercial API data such as ours to train their models. When a request goes through OpenRouter, OpenRouter does not keep its content, and the cloud provider hosting an Anthropic model keeps it no longer than Anthropic would. Requests that build your picks go to Google's Gemini model; Google may keep them for up to 55 days to detect abuse and does not use them to train its models.

We tell you this because "we deleted everything instantly" would be true of our own servers and misleading about the whole picture.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete → Delete my account.
  • Start over without deleting your account: Settings → Taste profile → Start my taste profile over.
  • Get a copy of your data, or correct it: write to hello@noomiapp.site and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, our legal basis is performance of the contract (giving you recommendations) and legitimate interest (keeping the service working and free of abuse). You have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or erase your data, and to complain to your local supervisory authority.

If you are in California, we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA.

Children

Noomi is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to hello@noomiapp.site and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
